## Initial design inferences, insight cards and personas

The secondary research has been formalized into seven attributed insight cards in [insight cards register](research/INSIGHT_CARDS.md) and three labelled simulated personas in [personas and journey maps](research/PERSONAS.md).

These are **design inferences** grounded in the supplied brief and named secondary sources, not interview results: (1) preserve cash and minimize registration burden; (2) comparable dated prices need provenance and uncertainty; (3) material aliases/low-literacy audio matter; (4) route/status distinctions prevent misleading “authorized” badges; (5) local persistence must survive connectivity loss; (6) receipts and dues need separate acknowledgement; (7) dataset quality is visible product value. Each card details evidence excerpt/paraphrase, source/locator, scope, inference, linked R-/T-/AT- IDs and counter-evidence/limitation without invented quotes.

Simulated persona A (Rajesh Kumar): Hindi-speaking independent collector using cash, approximate weight and intermittent data. Persona B (Santosh Shinde): Marathi-speaking collector needing local aliases/audio and route clarity. Persona C (Anil Verma): recycler receiving varied lots and needing measured-term confirmation/procurement records. No names, photos, village histories, incomes or participant counts imply real study participation. Scenario usability testing is validated with owner/devices as owner scenario tests.

## Claim table

| Allowed with evidence | Not established |
|---|---|
| “The prototype records these demonstrated events.” | “This proves actual recycling or compliance.” |
| “Indicative range from N dated comparable observations.” | “Official/fair guaranteed commodity price.” |
| “This facility record links to this dated registration evidence.” | “All imported businesses are current verified partners.” |
| “Model achieved measured metrics on this public test split.” | “Field accuracy is 90%” without field evaluation |
| “Illustrative input assumptions yield this delta.” | Guaranteed earnings uplift or ₹14,500→₹17,200 as actual results |
| “Desk research informed our design; fieldwork not conducted.” | Two collector interviews, photos or feedback that never happened |

Do not present national waste percentages, recycler counts, SIH entry counts, government formulae or market leadership from historical AI responses as facts. Find the primary source, date, denominator and scope before using a number. Keep slide-level citations and a claim/evidence ledger. Competitor comparison includes observed features, date and uncertainty; absence on a public page is not absence in a product. New statistical or regulatory claims require current primary-source verification.
