## Q&A crib

- **Offline confirmation?** Collector records evidence locally; server sync and authenticated recycler action confirm it. Unknown unsynced QR says pending, not verified.
- **Fair price?** Transparent dated comparable observations and a weighted range; confidence and sparse data visible. It is not an official guaranteed rate.
- **AI?** Trained advisory image model with recorded test performance; valuation/matching/anomaly rules are explainable statistical/rule systems, not claimed learned models.
- **Official certificate?** Platform handover/chain-of-custody record only. Applicable route registration belongs to the facility; received does not prove recycled.
- **Batteries?** Separate route, current suitable registration/material evidence, conservative safety; no unsafe dismantling advice.
- **Income/payment?** Cash supported; records are assertions and acknowledgements, not bank confirmation. Economics is hypothetical, no measured uplift.
- **Field research?** Desk research and owner device tests only; no primary collector interviews. The brief's field requirement remains unmet.
- **Other platforms?** Acknowledge existing actors; explain the demonstrated combination without unsupported exclusivity claims.
- **Scale/business model?** Prototype/free-plan limits are documented; hypothetical downstream fee and support costs are scenarios. Advanced integrations/ML belong to the tracked future backlog.
