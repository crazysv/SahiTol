# Problem statement and obligation mapping

Supplied identity: SIH26229, “Kabadiwala Connect – Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain,” Ministry of Mines / JNARDDC, Software, Clean & Green Technology. Product name: **SahiTol**.

The supplied full brief is preserved at `E:5336–5369` in [Entire_Content.txt](sources/Entire_Content.txt). The source labels it official/verbatim, but that label is an assertion in the attachment. During documentation the accessible [community mirror](https://sih2026.vuce.in/ps/SIH26229) explicitly described itself as unofficial; an authenticated official portal check remains Q01. Do not inherit the attachment's “verified today,” submission counts or cutoff as verified facts.

The supplied brief describes informal collectors' last-mile collection reach, information and institutional barriers, unsafe processing and loss of recoverable materials. It asks for a vernacular, low-literacy, offline-tolerant mobile platform for indicative price discovery, suitable authorized destinations, documented handover and payment records, making the formal channel convenient and economically worthwhile.
